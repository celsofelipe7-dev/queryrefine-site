FAQ

Common Questions

Does QueryRefine Pro collect any of my search data?
Absolutely not. QueryRefine Pro operates entirely inside your browser. Your queries never leave your device — the extension only modifies the URL parameters before Chrome sends them to Google. No analytics, no telemetry, no accounts.
Will it slow down my searches?
The extension uses Chrome's declarativeNetRequest API, which runs at the browser's C++ networking layer — not in JavaScript. This means zero added latency. In fact, filtering out irrelevant results may make your workflow faster overall.
Does it work with Google in other languages?
Yes. The extension targets www.google.com/search regardless of the hl= language parameter. The -site: operator is a universal Google search operator that works across all languages and regions.
Can I temporarily disable the extension?
Yes. Click the extension icon and hit "Pause Protection". This removes all active DNR rules instantly — your next search will be completely unfiltered. Hit "Resume Protection" to bring everything back.
What permissions does QueryRefine Pro need and why?
Only three minimal permissions:
  • storage — saves your filter preferences locally
  • activeTab — reloads the current Google tab when you toggle a filter
  • declarativeNetRequest — intercepts and rewrites Google search URLs
No broad host permissions beyond google.com/search.
